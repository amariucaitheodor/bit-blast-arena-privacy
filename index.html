Privacy Policy

Bit Blast: Arena — developed by Theodor A
Last updated: April 20, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Bit Blast: Arena. We take your privacy seriously and want to be upfront about what information is involved when you play our game.

This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected when you play Bit Blast: Arena on Android or iOS, how that data is used, and the choices you have. It applies to all platforms and versions of the game.

By downloading or playing Bit Blast: Arena, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

2. Information Collection and Use

Bit Blast: Arena is designed to collect as little data as possible. Here is what may be involved:

  • IP Address (temporary). When you use the online matchmaking feature, your device’s IP address is temporarily processed to establish a peer-to-peer or relay connection. This information is not stored after your session ends and is never associated with your identity.
  • Username. You may enter a display name before a match. This name is shared only with other players in the same session and is not linked to any account, email address, or personal identity.
  • Basic device information. In the event of a crash, we may collect your device model, operating system version, and a diagnostic error log to help us fix bugs. See “Log Data & Error Reporting” below for details.

We do not require account registration, do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location, and do not use behavioural advertising or tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

Some features of Bit Blast: Arena rely on third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing the data it collects.

Google Play Services. If you download the game from Google Play, Google’s standard platform services apply. Please review Google’s Privacy Policy for details on data Google may collect independently.

4. Log Data & Error Reporting

Whenever the game encounters an error or crash, it may automatically record diagnostic information (“Log Data”). This can include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Timestamp of the error
  • An error stack trace describing what went wrong
  • Your IP address at the time of the crash

This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. It is not shared with third parties except where a crash reporting service is used (see Third-Party Services above).

5. Children’s Privacy

Bit Blast: Arena is a game for all ages and has no minimum age requirement. We have designed the game with this in mind: it does not require account creation, does not ask for a real name or contact details, and does not display behavioural advertising.

In compliance with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent laws (e.g. GDPR-K in the EU), we do not knowingly collect personal information from any player. The only data processed during gameplay is:

  • A temporary IP address, held in memory for matchmaking only and discarded when the session ends
  • A player-chosen display name, shared with session participants only and never stored
  • Basic device diagnostics in the event of a crash (see Log Data & Error Reporting)

Because we do not collect persistent personal information, no parental consent mechanism is required for gameplay. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about your child’s use of the game, please contact us at the address in the “Contact Information” section and we will respond promptly.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary:

  • Session IP addresses are held in memory only for the duration of an active matchmaking session and are discarded when the session ends.
  • Crash / log data is retained for up to 90 days, after which it is permanently purged.

Requesting data deletion. If you would like to request the deletion of any data that may have been associated with your device, please email us at lakesidetheory@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. Include your device type and approximate date of play so we can locate any relevant records. We will process your request within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also include a note in the game’s update release notes on the app store.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Bit Blast: Arena after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
